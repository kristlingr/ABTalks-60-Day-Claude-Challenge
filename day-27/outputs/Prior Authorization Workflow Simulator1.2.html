Step Therapy Overview

Commonly called "fail-first" rules. Payers require patients to document trial and failure on lower-cost or conventional medications (like sulfasalazine) before advancing to specialized biologics.

Clinical Documentation Necessity

Insurance review teams confirm diagnosis codes match evidence in clinic chart files. Missing clinic notes are the leading cause of administrative prior authorization delays and denials.

System Metrics Tracked

Modern health systems track operational metrics such as total denial rate, successful appeal percentage, and average clinician resolution time to reduce patient access delays.
