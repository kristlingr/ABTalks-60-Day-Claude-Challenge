What this tool does

This is your Personal AI Playbook — a private, local library of reusable AI prompt systems, tailored to how you actually work (data analysis, prototyping, coding, and iteration). Instead of hunting for the "right" prompt every time, you save modular, editable templates once and reuse them. Everything is stored only in this browser (local storage) — nothing is sent anywhere.

What's an "autonomous loop"? It's a prompt that tells the AI to keep checking and improving its own answer against criteria you set, instead of stopping after one draft — closer to "work on this until it's actually right" than "answer once."     What is the Personal AI Playbook?

    This is a local, personal library of reusable AI prompt systems — built around how a data/AI specialist actually works day to day. Instead of rewriting prompts from scratch or digging through old chats, you build modular templates once and reuse them forever.

    A quick overview: how many workflows you've saved, and what you used most recently.

    Your library of saved prompt templates, grouped into categories relevant to your work (data analysis, prototyping, coding & debugging, iteration, daily tasks). Each has editable variables, an example, and best practices.

    Assemble a one-off prompt from labeled building blocks (role, objective, context, constraints, reasoning strategy, output format, tone, examples, quality checks) with a live preview as you go.

    Turns any prompt into a self-checking, iterative version — the AI evaluates its own output, revises it, and stops only when your conditions are met.

    A simple recurring-task tracker for things you do with AI assistance often, optionally linked to a saved workflow.

    Everything is stored in this browser's local storage only — nothing leaves your machine, and closing the tab won't lose your data.
