Administrative Mechanics Guide
Step Therapy Requirements
Insurers often enforce formulary restrictions requiring cheaper therapies (e.g., generic medications) before approving advanced biologic or specialty procedures.
Administrative Pends vs. Denials
A "Pend" is a temporary request for information with a short completion window (typically 72 hours). Failing to upload the requested file leads to a hard Clinical Denial.
Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Reviews
If denied, the ordering clinician can discuss the clinical nuances directly with a medical director. These discussions must often occur within 15 business days.
