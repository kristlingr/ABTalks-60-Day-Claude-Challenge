# Day 12

# Key Learnings:
Business understanding is as important as technical expertise.
The best models solve business problems, not just prediction tasks.
Feature engineering often creates more value than changing algorithms.
Better data consistently beats more complex models.
Machine Learning is only one part of the solution.
Data pipelines, APIs, automation, and monitoring are what make models useful in production.
Domain knowledge accelerates innovation.
Working across healthcare, fraud, and financial analytics has shown me that context drives better decisions.
Continuous learning is the biggest competitive advantage.
The data ecosystem evolves every day, and staying curious is the key to building impactful AI solutions.



Data → Insights → Automation → Impact.
That's the journey I'm committed to building, one project at a time.
