# Day 2: Prompt Engineering for Beginners

## Section 1: What is Prompt Engineering?
Prompt Engineering means writing instructions for AI in a clear, specific, and helpful way so the AI can give a better answer. Instead of typing something vague, you tell the AI what you want, what format you want, and any important details it should follow.

### Why it matters:
The quality of the answer depends a lot on the quality of the prompt. A clear prompt helps Claude understand your goal faster, reduces confusion, and gives you a result that is more accurate and useful.

### Practical Benefits:
1. Save time with fewer retries.
2. More accurate and useful responses.
3. Better control over output style and tone.

## Section 2: Weak vs Improved Prompt Example

**Weak Prompt:**
'Tell me about marketing.'

**Improved Prompt:**
'Explain digital marketing for a complete beginner in simple language. Include a short definition, 3 main types of digital marketing, and one example for each. Keep the answer under 150 words and use a friendly tone.'
