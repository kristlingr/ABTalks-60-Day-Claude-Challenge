CMS Observation Status — 2-Midnight Rule
CMS 2-Midnight Rule applies — Observation status carries different cost-sharing, SNF eligibility, and billing requirements than inpatient admission. Medicare patients require written MOON (Medicare Outpatient Observation Notice) notification within 36 hours of observation placement. Observation patients cannot access the SNF benefit during or following the stay.
