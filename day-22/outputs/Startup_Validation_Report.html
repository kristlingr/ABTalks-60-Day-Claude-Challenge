Bottom Line

The idea addresses a real, underserved pain point that the founder has personally experienced. India’s cloud kitchen market is growing at 12–17% CAGR, and late-night delivery is a proven revenue driver (20–30% of orders for some operators). However, generic "food for anyone anytime" positioning is too broad — success depends on narrowing to "trusted hygienic meals for IT night shift workers" and proving unit economics before quitting a day job or raising capital.
