Quarterly Growth Strategy Meeting

Meeting transcript · Q2 performance review · Topics: revenue, conversion, engineering capacity, retention, hiring, conference

📊 Summary
Q2 revenue grew 12%, falling short of the 18% annual target. Five enterprise deals slipped to next quarter due to procurement delays and security review requests. Engineering is near capacity with the August analytics dashboard release. A website redesign is proposed to improve conversion rates, but cost estimates are pending. Customer retention is strong at 94%, though support ticket volume has increased — primarily due to reporting performance issues already flagged as a known engineering problem. Hiring decisions and the customer conference budget are both deferred pending further data.
