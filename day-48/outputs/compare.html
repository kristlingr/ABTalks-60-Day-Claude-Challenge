    How this was researched

    ▸

    The scope changed mid-research. The brief originally asked for the BMW 520i and 520d. As of July 2026, BMW India sells the current-generation (G60, launched July 2024) 5 Series in a single configuration: the 530Li M Sport petrol, long-wheelbase, no diesel offered. The 520i/520d names belong to the previous generation or to overseas markets and are not on sale in India today. Rather than inventing pricing or mileage for discontinued trims, this comparison uses the 530Li as BMW's actual current offering, set against the Mercedes-Benz E 200 (petrol) and E 220d (diesel), both of which remain on sale.

    Price conflicts resolved: Ex-showroom prices for both cars vary by ₹2–4 lakh across CarDekho, ZigWheels, Autocar India and CarWale, largely because of city-specific RTO/GST timing and dealer-reported "starting" prices. This tool uses each source's most recently updated (July 2026) Delhi ex-showroom figure, since it's the most consistent basis across all three cars.
    Fuel efficiency is not variant-split for the E-Class: ARAI/company-claimed mileage for the E-Class is published by most Indian outlets as a single "12–15 kmpl" band covering all engines, not broken out per E 200 / E 220d. This tool assigns the petrol E 200 the lower end (12 kmpl) and the diesel E 220d the upper end (15 kmpl), consistent with team-bhp's reported 15 kmpl figure and typical petrol/diesel spread — but treat both as ⚠ estimates, not ARAI-certified per-variant numbers.
    BMW's Euro NCAP score was measured on the electric i5, not the 530Li petrol: Euro NCAP's 2023/24 five-star test of the G60-generation 5 Series body was conducted on the i5 eDrive40 electric variant, since that's the configuration Euro NCAP selected that cycle. The overall 5-star rating and body structure apply across the G60 range, but the exact percentage breakdown (89% Adult / 85% Child / 86% VRU / 78% Safety Assist) reflects the electric car's weight distribution and battery-pack structure, not the petrol 530Li specifically. This is flagged in the table.

    Safety ratings for the Mercedes-Benz E-Class (W214) come from Euro NCAP's own 2024 test and datasheet, where it was named Euro NCAP's overall "Best Performer 2024" — the highest score of any car tested that year, applicable to the same bodyshell sold in India.

    Warranty terms come directly from Mercedes-Benz India's and BMW India's own after-sales pages, plus corroborating consumer-guide summaries (Spinny, CarWale, Team-BHP). Both brands offer extended-warranty add-ons beyond their standard term; only the standard, no-cost factory term is scored, with the extension ceiling noted for context.
