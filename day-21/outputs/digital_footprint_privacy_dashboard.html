Privacy improvement plan
1
Audit app permissions this week
Go to Settings → Privacy → Location / Camera / Contacts on your device. Revoke permissions for any app that doesn't strictly need them. TikTok, Snapchat, and Roblox are likely over-permissioned.
2
Opt out of ad personalisation on Google & Meta
Visit myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy and Facebook's Ad Preferences to limit cross-site tracking. This reduces your adtech footprint significantly — estimate.
3
Enable Google Photos face grouping opt-out
Google Photos uses facial recognition to group people. In the app: Account → Photos Settings → Group Similar Faces → toggle off if you prefer not to participate.
4
Review TikTok data settings
Under Privacy → Personalization & Data, limit data collection. Consider whether the app's data practices align with your comfort level — particularly for cross-app tracking.
5
Use separate browsers for search vs shopping
Segregating activity prevents cross-context profiling. Use Firefox with uBlock Origin for general browsing, and a separate session for Google-linked activity.
6
Enable two-factor authentication everywhere
Your wide surface area makes account takeover higher-risk. Enable 2FA on Google, Amazon, Meta, Discord, and Spotify. Use an authenticator app, not SMS, where possible.
